# Heroes' Arsenal — the big list (plan)

**Status:** the weapons (section 5) and the new attacks are **done in Milestone 17a (v0.17.0)**. The ability
book, the 6-slot bar and the abilities for **levels 2–30 are done in Milestone 17b (v0.18.0)**. The abilities for
**levels 32–64, the 20 shared spells and the animations are done in Milestone 17c (v0.19.0)**. The abilities for
levels 66–100 and the ultimates are **NOT IMPLEMENTED yet** — they come in M17d.
Change, remove or add anything before we build it.

What is in this list:
- **50 new abilities for every class** (200 in total). Each class also keeps its 3 abilities from today,
  so every class has 53.
- **20 new weapons**, including 6 new weapon types: bow, spear, war hammer, greatsword, wand and tome.
- **20 new shared spells** that any class can learn from a tome.

## How it works in the game

- **You get one new ability every 2 levels**, from level 2 to level 100. When you level up, the new ability
  is added to your ability book.
- **Active** abilities go on your ability bar. **Passive** abilities are always on once you have learned them.
- **The ability bar has 6 slots for abilities and 2 slots for spells**, so you choose your build. You change
  your bar at a bed or a campfire, which makes the choice matter.
- **The class talent stays (Option A).** The class is still a little better with its own weapons and skills.
- **Abilities use the class resource.** The Barbarian uses Rage. The Knight, Wizard and Assassin use Mana.
- Words used below:
  - *Stun*: the enemy can't act.
  - *Slow*: the enemy moves slower.
  - *Chill*: a slow that freezes on the second chill.
  - *Bleed* and *poison*: damage over time.
  - *Mark*: a target that takes extra damage.

## How big is this? (honest estimate)

This list is about **5 times bigger than any milestone so far**. Each ability needs code, balance numbers,
an effect you can see, an icon, a sound and tests. Building it all at once would take a very long time and
nothing could be tested along the way. So I suggest building it in steps, and every step is a working game:

1. **M17a: Weapons.** ✅ Done (v0.17.0).
   - The 6 new weapon types, all 20 weapons and arrows.
   - The new attacks: charged heavy, sprint attack, plunging attack and riposte.
2. **M17b: The ability book.** ✅ Done (v0.18.0).
   - The 6+2 slot bar, choosing abilities at a bed or campfire, and passives.
   - Abilities for levels 2–30 (15 per class).
3. **M17c: Abilities for levels 32–64** ✅ Done (v0.19.0), with the animations (17 per class) **and the 20 shared spells.**
4. **M17d: Abilities for levels 66–100** (18 per class), **the ultimates, and the new animations.**
