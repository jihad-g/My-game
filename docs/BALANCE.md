## Economy

- Shops sell at ×1.35 of base value and buy at ×0.45 (before region, reputation and saturation).
- Best town-to-town trade (cheapest region + best reputation → dearest region + kingdom + best
  reputation): **×0.84** of the money spent - trading never makes money on its own.
- Selling the same item repeatedly saturates a shop (−6% per item, down to ×0.4).
